01. About these terms

These terms apply when you buy from Marauder, when you write to us, and when you use this site. They are written to be read once, in plain English, without a second document to decode them.

Marauder is a catalogue of homeware run from a studio in Lisbon. Where these terms say "we", they mean the studio; where they say "you", they mean the person placing the order. If something here is unclear, write to us before you order rather than after.

02. Orders and payment

An order is an offer, and it becomes a contract when we confirm it by email. Until then, we may decline an order — most often because an object sold out between your click and our confirmation, or because the delivery address is outside the area we ship to.

Prices are shown in US dollars and include the work, the packing and the handling. Shipping is calculated separately and shown before payment. Sales tax or import duty is the buyer's responsibility where it applies.

We accept card payments through a payment provider; we never see or store your card number. If a payment is declined, the order is not created and nothing is charged.

03. Shipping

We ship from Lisbon across the European Union and the United Kingdom. Orders leave the studio within three to five working days. Shipping is free above $120; below that it is a flat $9.

Each object is wrapped in paper and board, with no plastic. If a parcel arrives damaged, photograph the box before opening it fully and write to us within seven days. We replace or refund damaged objects; we do not ask you to prove the courier was at fault.

Delivery times depend on the destination and the courier. We will send a tracking reference when the parcel leaves the studio.

04. Returns and exchanges

You may return an unused object within 30 days of delivery, in its original packaging. Write to us first so we know what is coming back. Return shipping is paid by the buyer unless the object arrived damaged or is not what you ordered.

Once the object is back in the studio and we have checked it, we refund the price of the object within ten working days. Original shipping is refunded only when the return is due to our error.

Objects made to order, and candles that have been lit, cannot be returned. Nothing in this section limits your statutory rights as a consumer in the European Union.

05. Privacy

We collect what we need to send an order and answer a message: your name, email, delivery address and the contents of what you write. We do not sell data, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not send a newsletter unless you ask for one.

Contact form submissions are kept for 24 months so that a conversation can be picked up again, then deleted. Order records are kept for the period required by Portuguese accounting law. Our payment provider and courier receive only the fields they need to do their part.

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for a copy, or ask us to delete it. Write to hello@marauder.example and we will answer within 30 days.

06. Intellectual property

The photographs, text, product names and the Marauder wordmark on this site belong to the studio or are used with permission. You may share a link, quote a paragraph with attribution, or save a photograph for your own reference. You may not resell our photographs, use them in your own catalogue, or present our words as yours.

The objects themselves are made by ceramists and weavers we work with. Their forms are theirs; our photographs of them are ours.

07. Liability

We are responsible for an object arriving as described and in working condition. We are not responsible for damage caused by using an object outside its care notes — a stoneware plate taken from the freezer to a hot oven, a vessel left standing with water for a month.

Our liability for any order is limited to the amount you paid for it. This does not exclude liability for death, personal injury or fraud, which cannot be limited by a document like this one.

If a page on this site is wrong about a price or a dimension, we will tell you before charging and you may cancel without cost.

08. Changes to these terms

We may change these terms when the way we work changes. The version and the date at the top of this page always show which text is current, and we keep earlier versions on file.

A change applies to orders placed after it is published, not to an order already confirmed. If a change is significant, we will note it at the top of the page for 30 days rather than quietly replacing the text.

That is the whole document. If one sentence here decides whether you order, tell us which one and we will answer it directly, in a message written by a person. Talk to us.
