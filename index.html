02 — How we choose

A short catalogue, on purpose.

Importance is not a matter of how much we sell, but of how little we are willing to list.

  1. 01

    Small on purpose

    Six objects, not six hundred. We add a few each season and let a few go, so that choosing stays a decision rather than a scroll.

  2. 02

    Made to be kept

    Stoneware that survives a dishwasher, linen that softens instead of wearing out. If it would not last ten years in our own kitchen, we do not list it.

  3. 03

    Technology that stays quiet

    No scores, no badges, no claims. The model reads five answers and puts three objects in front of you. The choosing is still yours.

What the model does

The model was trained on pairs of objects that couples kept together for more than a year. It reads five short answers — the room, the light, the material you reach for, how you host, how often you move — and returns three objects that already sat side by side in other homes. It does not rank you, guess your taste from an email, or change the price. It only helps you start somewhere.
