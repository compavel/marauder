The Pairing Box

$120

Three objects our model pairs most often, packed in one box: a pair of tumblers, a washed linen runner, and a candle. It began as an internal test — which pieces do couples keep side by side — and became the thing people ask for when they cannot decide. The box is printed on uncoated board and closed with paper tape.

Ships in 3–5 working days. Free above $120. Shipping and returns
